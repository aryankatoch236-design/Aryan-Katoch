From Aryan's desk

Why local marketing so often disappoints, and what I do differently

Most local business owners I meet have already tried marketing. A page manager who posted a few graphics a week. A printer who ran off a thousand pamphlets. An ad that got likes but no calls. The money went out and very little came back through the door.

The problem usually isn't effort. It's that online and offline are handled by different people who never talk to each other. The ads have nothing to do with what's happening in the shop, and the pamphlets have no way of telling you whether anyone read them.

At Arvinex we plan the two together. Your Google listing, reels and local ads build attention. Your pamphlets, shop board and on-ground offers build trust in the neighbourhood. And we track what matters to you: calls, WhatsApp messages, walk-ins and offer redemptions.

"Likes don't pay rent. I'd rather report ten real enquiries than ten thousand impressions."Aryan Katoch

The online + offline approach

Online gets their attention.
Offline earns their trust.

Picture one customer over one week. She sees your reel on Instagram. She searches and finds you on Google Maps with good reviews. A pamphlet with an offer lands in her hand at the market. On Saturday she walks past your stall. By then you're not one option among many; you're the one she already knows.

  1. MonSees your reelOnline
  2. WedFinds you on MapsOnline
  3. ThuGets your pamphletOffline
  4. SatWalks inOffline

A typical first 90 days

  1. Days 1–7

    Look and listen

    A call, a visit, and a review of your Google listing, Instagram and nearby competitors. You get a short written audit.

  2. Days 8–14

    Set things up

    Fix your Google profile, plan the first reels, design pamphlets and counter material, set up WhatsApp replies.

  3. Days 15–45

    Launch together

    Ads, reels, pamphlet drops and any on-ground activity go live in the same weeks, so each one supports the others.

  4. Days 46–90

    Review and grow

    We report what came in, drop what didn't work, ask happy customers for reviews and build on what did.

Timelines are a guide. Your written plan will say what fits your business and budget.

FAQ

Questions owners ask

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you need and the size of your area. Small budgets are welcome. Tell me your range in the form, and after the free audit I'll send a written quote.

Can you guarantee Google rankings or sales?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. Rankings and sales depend on things nobody fully controls. We commit to the work we'll do and to reporting clearly on what happened.

How soon will I see results?

Offers and pamphlets can bring enquiries within days. A Google profile and a reel following take weeks to build. Your plan will say what to expect for your business.

Do I have to do both online and offline?

No. You can start with just one. We'll tell you honestly if we think the other would help, and why.

Do I need a website?

Not always. For many local shops a complete Google listing and WhatsApp are enough to start. Clinics, coaching centres and homestays usually benefit from a simple site.

Is there a long contract?

No long lock-in. Monthly plans can be stopped with notice, and single campaigns end when the campaign ends.

Do you work outside Kangra?

Online work, yes, anywhere. For printing and on-ground activity we focus on towns we can reach in person; ask us about yours.

What do you need from me?

A short call, access to your Google and Instagram pages if you have them, and some time for photos or a video shoot.
